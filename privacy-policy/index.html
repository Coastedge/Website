Privacy Policy

Coast Edge Electrical Pty Ltd · Last updated 31 July 2026

Coast Edge Electrical Pty Ltd ("we", "us") is a licensed QLD electrical contractor (Lic No. 1511275) servicing the Sunshine Coast and surrounds. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy explains what we collect on this website, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Information you give us — when you use our quote or contact forms we collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb, and any job details you include.
  • Information collected automatically — like most websites, we and our service providers may collect your IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and how you found us (for example, via a Google ad or search).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, and deliver our electrical services.
  • To contact you about your job by phone or email.
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve it.
  • For advertising measurement and remarketing (see below).

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • Google Analytics — to measure site traffic and usage. Data is aggregated and doesn't identify you personally. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Ads & remarketing — we may run Google advertising, which uses cookies to measure conversions (for example, that an enquiry came from an ad) and to show our ads to people who have visited this site before. You can manage this at Google Ads Settings.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Who we share it with

Your information is only shared with the service providers that make this website and our business run — for example our website hosting (Vercel), email services, and Google (analytics and advertising as described above). These providers may store data outside Australia. We only share what's needed for them to do their job.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to handle your job and our business records obligations.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you — email admin@coastedge.com.au or call 0421 165 502. If you have a privacy concern we can't resolve, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — the latest version will always be on this page.
